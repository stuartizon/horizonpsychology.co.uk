When you send an enquiry, the details you provide are used only to respond to you and, if you go ahead, to arrange and deliver sessions. Enquiries that do not lead to work together are deleted.

Enquiries from the contact form are delivered by Cloudflare, which also hosts this website. Cloudflare receives what you enter in the form, which is your name, email address, what you are enquiring about, whether you would prefer sessions online or face-to-face, and your message, and sends it to Dr Izon by email. The website itself does not store or log what you send. Enquiries, whether sent through the form or by email, arrive in Dr Izon's email account, which is provided by Google.

Clinical records are kept securely, are accessible only to Dr Izon, and are held for the period set out in professional guidance. You may ask to see the information held about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted where there is no legal reason to keep it.

No information is sold or shared for marketing. Where a third-party service is used to deliver sessions or store records, it is chosen for its security and data protection standards.
