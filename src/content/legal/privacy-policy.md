Dr Emma Izon is the data controller for Horizon Psychology. As a sole practitioner, she is responsible for determining how and why your personal information is processed.

## 2. Information we collect and use

The personal information collected depends on the services you access and your individual circumstances. Only information relevant to the purpose for which it is collected will be requested.

### Enquiries and contact forms

When you contact Horizon Psychology, the information you provide is used to respond to your enquiry and, if you decide to proceed, to arrange and deliver the relevant services.

This includes your name, email address, the service you are enquiring about, your preference for online or face-to-face sessions, and the details of your message.

### Therapy clients

When you access psychological therapy, information collected may include:

- Your name, date of birth and contact details.
- Relevant health information, clinical history and information shared during sessions.
- Clinical notes, assessments, treatment plans and records of progress.
- GP or other healthcare provider details, where appropriate.
- Emergency contact information, where appropriate.
- Insurance details, if you use an insurer to fund your sessions.
- Appointment, payment and invoicing information.

This information is used to provide psychological care, maintain appropriate clinical records, manage appointments and payments, support clinical decision-making and coordinate care where necessary and appropriate.

For couples therapy, information may relate to both individuals and will be handled in accordance with applicable confidentiality and data protection requirements.

### Clinical and research supervision

For clinical supervision, information may include your contact details, professional background, supervision notes, discussions about clinical practice and records of agreed actions.

For research supervision, information may include your contact details, research proposals, project discussions, research materials and records of supervision.

Where clinical cases or research participants are discussed, identifying information should be minimised wherever possible, in accordance with relevant professional, ethical and data protection responsibilities.

### Online sessions and recordings

Online therapy and supervision sessions delivered through Zoom or Google Meet are not recorded by Horizon Psychology. Audio and video recordings are not made, and sessions are not transcribed using AI tools. Information shared during sessions is treated confidentially and handled in accordance with this Privacy Policy and the [Horizon Psychology Confidentiality Statement](/confidentiality/).

### Payments, queries and complaints

Payment and appointment information may be collected to manage bookings, process payments, maintain appropriate financial records and respond to queries or [complaints](/complaints/).

Where a concern, complaint or legal matter arises, relevant records and communications may be retained and reviewed where necessary to investigate the matter, meet legal or professional obligations, or establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

## 3. How enquiry and client information is collected, used and stored

### Enquiries and contact forms

The Horizon Psychology website uses Cloudflare to host the website and deliver contact form submissions. When you submit the contact form, Cloudflare receives the information you enter and transmits it to Dr Emma Izon by email.

The website itself does not store or log the contents of your submission. This relates to the contents of the form and does not necessarily mean that the website or its providers retain no technical or security logs.

Enquiries sent through the contact form or directly by email are received in Dr Izon's email account, which is provided by Google. Your information is used to respond to you, manage appointments and, where applicable, establish arrangements for therapy or supervision.

Enquiries that do not lead to an ongoing professional relationship are retained for six months from the date of last contact, unless a concern, complaint or other legitimate reason or legal obligation requires a longer retention period.

### Clinical and supervision records

Where you become a therapy client or supervision participant, information relevant to the service is maintained in appropriate clinical or professional records.

Full, clear and accurate records are maintained in accordance with applicable professional requirements. Records may include session notes, assessments, relevant health information, treatment plans, supervision discussions and records of communications relating to your care or supervision.

Clinical and supervision records are stored securely, with access restricted to Dr Izon and any authorised service providers who need to process information to support the operation of the practice. Information may also be disclosed where required or justified by law, professional obligations or safeguarding responsibilities.

Records are retained in accordance with the retention periods set out in [Section 7](#7-how-long-we-keep-your-information).

## 5. Lawful bases for processing

UK data protection law requires a lawful basis for processing personal information. Where special category data, such as health information, is involved, an additional legal condition must also apply.

Depending on the circumstances, Horizon Psychology may rely on the following lawful bases under [Article 6 of the UK GDPR](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2016/679/article/6):

- **Article 6(1)(b) – Contract:** To take steps before entering into an agreement and to fulfil arrangements for therapy or supervision.
- **Article 6(1)(c) – Legal obligation:** To comply with applicable legal or regulatory requirements.
- **Article 6(1)(f) – Legitimate interests:** To manage enquiries, maintain appropriate records, administer the practice, understand how the website is used, and address complaints or legal claims, where these interests do not override your rights.
- **Article 6(1)(d) – Vital interests:** Where necessary to protect someone's life or other vital interests.

For special category personal information, the relevant condition under [Article 9](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2016/679/article/9) may include:

- **Article 9(2)(h) – Health or social care:** Where necessary for the provision of psychological assessment, therapy and care, or the management of health services, and the relevant legal requirements are met.
- **Article 9(2)(c) – Vital interests:** Where necessary to protect vital interests and the legal conditions for relying on this provision are met.
- **Article 9(2)(g) – Substantial public interest:** Where a specific condition under UK law applies, such as certain safeguarding circumstances.

The appropriate lawful basis and condition depend on the purpose and circumstances of the processing. Not every basis applies to every activity.

Your information is treated as confidential and is not routinely shared with third parties without an appropriate lawful basis.

## 7. How long we keep your information

Horizon Psychology retains personal information only for as long as necessary, taking account of professional guidance, legal obligations and the purpose for which the information was collected.

The following retention periods apply:

- **Clients and supervisees:** Records are retained for seven years from the date of last contact.
- **Enquiries that do not proceed:** Information is retained for six months from the date of last contact where the individual does not access services, become a client or supervisee, enter into an agreement, or raise a concern or complaint.
- **Concerns and complaints:** Records relating to a concern or complaint about Horizon Psychology or a person associated with the practice are retained for seven years from the date the concern or complaint is mutually resolved.

These periods are intended to support appropriate record keeping, professional responsibilities and the management of enquiries or complaints. Information may need to be retained for longer where a legal obligation or other justified circumstance applies.

When information is no longer necessary to retain, it will be securely deleted or disposed of.

## 8. Your data protection rights

Under UK data protection law, you may have the right to:

- **Access:** Request a copy of your personal information and information about how it is used.
- **Rectification:** Ask for inaccurate or incomplete information to be corrected.
- **Erasure:** Request deletion of your information in certain circumstances.
- **Restriction:** Ask for the use of your information to be restricted in certain circumstances.
- **Object:** Object to particular types of processing.
- **Data portability:** Request the transfer of certain information you have provided, where the legal requirements apply.
- **Withdraw consent:** Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

These rights are subject to applicable legal conditions and exemptions. For example, clinical records may need to be retained where there is a valid legal or professional reason to do so.

To exercise your rights, please contact [hello@horizonpsychology.co.uk](mailto:hello@horizonpsychology.co.uk).

Requests will be handled without undue delay and generally within one calendar month. In certain circumstances, the law permits an extension, for example where a request is complex. If an extension is needed, you will be informed within the applicable timeframe.

## 9. How your information is protected

Appropriate security measures are used to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, alteration or disclosure.

Access to clinical and professional records is restricted to those who need the information for legitimate purposes. Third-party providers are considered in relation to their security and data protection arrangements.

No method of electronic storage or communication can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but reasonable and appropriate safeguards are taken to protect your information.

## 10. Confidentiality after death

The duty of confidentiality continues after a person's death. Although UK data protection law generally applies to living individuals, professional standards and the common law duty of confidentiality may continue to apply.

Requests for access to records after death will be considered carefully and in accordance with applicable legal requirements, including the Access to Health Records Act 1990 where relevant.

You may express preferences about how your information is shared, including after your death. These wishes will be taken into account wherever possible. However, confidentiality cannot be guaranteed in all circumstances, and information may be disclosed where there is a legal obligation or another lawful justification.

## 11. Cookies and website usage

Horizon Psychology does not use cookies on its website, and does not store anything on your device to track you.

To understand how the website is used and to improve it, Horizon Psychology counts visits to the website using Umami, a privacy-focused analytics service, with the information stored in the European Union. Umami records:

- the pages visited, and the website or search engine a visitor arrived from
- the type of browser, operating system and device, and the screen size and language setting
- an approximate location, such as the country or town, worked out from the connection
- when an enquiry is sent, or cannot be sent, through the contact form, and when the email address is clicked

Umami does not record anything you write in the contact form, and it does not keep IP addresses or any other information that identifies you. Visits are counted anonymously and cannot be linked to you. The information is used only to understand how the website is used and to improve it, and Umami does not use it for any other purpose. It is kept for six months.

You can stop your visits being counted at any time:

- using the switch below, which keeps a note in your browser not to count your visits, until you clear your browser's data
- by turning on your browser's Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control setting
- by using a browser or extension that blocks analytics
