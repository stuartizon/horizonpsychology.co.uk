title: Complaints procedure
lead: If something about your care has not been right, please say so. Concerns are taken seriously and are welcomed.
draft: true
---

The first step is to raise it directly, either in a session or by email. Most concerns can be resolved by talking them through. You will receive an acknowledgement within 5 working days and a full response within 20 working days.

If you would prefer not to raise it directly, or you are not satisfied with the response, you can contact the Health and Care Professions Council (HCPC), which regulates practising clinical psychologists, or the British Association for Behavioural and Cognitive Psychotherapies (BABCP).

Making a complaint will not affect the care you receive.
