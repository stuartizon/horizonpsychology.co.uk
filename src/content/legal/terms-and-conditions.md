Fees are payable in advance of each session. If you need to cancel or reschedule, at least 48 hours' notice is asked for so the time can be offered to someone else. Cancellations with less than 48 hours' notice are charged at 50% of the session fee; cancellations with less than 24 hours' notice, or missed appointments without notice, are charged in full.

The free 15-minute introductory call carries no charge and no obligation to continue.
